About CONSCIENTIA

A Team Built on Decades of Expertise

Multidisciplinary PhD-level professionals dedicated to advancing sustainable development through evidence-based solutions.

Our Foundation

Who We Are

CONSCIENTIA Training, Consultancy and Research PLC was founded in August 2012 by senior experts with over 20 years of individual experience in research, teaching, and field consultancy.

Our team draws on deep specializations across climate change, forestry, policy and institutional systems, GIS, smart agriculture, and natural resource economics.

We work closely with universities and research centers to generate knowledge, inform policy, and deliver practical, scalable solutions throughout Ethiopia and the wider region.

  • Partnership first a reliable ally in efforts that make a difference
  • Integrity & punctuality commitments honored without exception
  • Tailored solutions adapted to each client's specific context
  • Continuous feedback solutions refined through ongoing dialogue
  • Knowledge generation research at the core of everything we do
"We believe that lasting change in forests and communities comes from combining rigorous knowledge with genuine partnership."

Areas of Expertise

What Our Team Brings

Integrating science, business, innovation, and policy to advance climate resilience and sustainable development in Ethiopia and the Horn of Africa.

🌲

Forestry, Climate & Ecosystem Management

Climate-smart forestry, plantation development, sustainable forest management, REDD+, carbon sequestration, carbon markets, climate finance, MRV systems, forest landscape restoration, sustainable forest enterprises, forest product value chains and marketing, forest-based business development, and forest policy and law.

🌾

Climate-Smart Agriculture & Natural Resource Management

Climate-resilient agriculture, agroforestry, drought adaptation, soil and water conservation, smart irrigation, sustainable land management, watershed management, rangeland management, ecosystem services, community-based natural resource management, and sustainable livelihood development.

🌎

Climate Change, Policy & Governance

Climate science, climate variability and impacts, greenhouse gas accounting, vulnerability and risk assessment, climate adaptation and mitigation, NDCs, NAPs, low-carbon development pathways, climate finance, institutional strengthening, climate governance, and international climate negotiations.

🦋

Biodiversity, Wildlife & Ecotourism

Biodiversity assessment and conservation, protected area management, wildlife management, habitat restoration, ecosystem conservation, ecotourism development, nature-based solutions, and integration of biodiversity considerations into land-use planning and development programs.

🗺

Renewable Energy, GIS & Spatial Analytics

Renewable energy planning and development, solar, wind and biomass energy systems, rural electrification, energy efficiency, remote sensing, GIS mapping, land-use and land-cover analysis, spatial planning, landscape assessment, surface water and watershed analysis, and environmental monitoring systems.

📋

Project Development, Monitoring and Evaluation

Project identification and design, feasibility studies, business plan development, investment proposal preparation, results-based management, monitoring and evaluation (M&E), impact assessment, carbon accounting, knowledge management, capacity building, training, and technical advisory services.
