Our Foundation

Who We Are

CONSCIENTIA Training, Consultancy and Research PLC was founded in August 2012 by senior experts with over 20 years of individual experience in research, teaching, and field consultancy.

Our team draws on deep specializations across climate change, forestry, policy and institutional systems, GIS, smart agriculture, and natural resource economics.

We work closely with universities and research centers to generate knowledge, inform policy, and deliver practical, scalable solutions throughout Ethiopia and the wider region.

  • Partnership first a reliable ally in efforts that make a difference
  • Integrity & punctuality commitments honored without exception
  • Tailored solutions adapted to each client's specific context
  • Continuous feedback solutions refined through ongoing dialogue
  • Knowledge generation research at the core of everything we do
